Before you record

Your history, your choices.

A screen history can contain private information. Understand what is saved and where it goes before recording your normal day.

Start with a harmless test session. This is an early release with unencrypted history and known privacy gaps. Read what gets recorded before using it on your everyday desktop.

What gets saved

Changed-screen screenshots, text recognized from those images, timestamps and available application/window metadata. rsRewind does not record audio or keystrokes. Metadata availability differs by platform; the Mac preview does not currently report a foreground application.

Recording, recognition and search run on your computer. No account or telemetry is required. History is unencrypted at rest; normal OS account permissions and any disk encryption you configure are its protection.

Controls you can use

Pause or stop

Use the tray or menu bar and check recorder state. Closing the viewer leaves recording running. Known pause and enforcement gaps remain; these controls are not a guarantee against every sensitive frame.

Exclude apps and windows

Rules are intended to skip a whole monitor when a visible matching window is present. Suggested defaults are primarily Windows/Linux patterns; verify your own apps, especially on the Mac preview.

Keep less

Defaults retain 90 days with a 50 GB soft limit. Change retention and exclusions in config.toml. Deleting history does not erase copies you already exported or backed up.

Configure exclusions carefully

Review existing process and window-title lists before editing: replacing a list replaces its defaults. App naming varies by platform. Test with synthetic content, and do not rely on suggested password-manager or private-window patterns as universal protection.

When history can leave your computer

Exporting creates files for manual transfer to another computer. Optional MCP assistant access in development builds returns history to the connected assistant, whose provider may receive it. MCP is off by default, with a separate opt-in for screenshots.

Import checks integrity, not who sent the file. Only import files from sources you trust. Multi-machine import has not had an independent security review.

What “forget” means

Forgetting recent history removes its screenshots, recognized text and window titles from the active store. Backups, exported segments and other computers’ copies can still contain that history. Uninstalling preserves your history folder; it is not a deletion action.
